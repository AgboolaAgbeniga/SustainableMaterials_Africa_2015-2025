# Project Architecture & Methodology: Sustainable Building Materials in Africa (2015–2025)

## Overview
This repository contains a Systematic / Rapid Review titled:
**"Sustainable Building Materials in Africa (2015–2025): A Rapid Systematic Review"** (Author: Agbeniga Agboola), registered/structured for the Open Science Framework (OSF) and designed according to PRISMA 2020 guidelines.

## Research Objective
To synthesize research and evidence on sustainable building materials (SBMs) studied or applied in Africa over the decade 2015–2025 across:
- **Material Classes:** Earth-based (e.g., compressed earth blocks, rammed earth, laterite), Bio-based (bamboo, timber, hempcrete, straw bale), Recycled/Waste-based (recycled aggregate, plastic waste bricks), Low-carbon binders/cements (LC3, calcined clay, geopolymers), and Smart/Innovative materials.
- **Sustainability Dimensions:** Environmental, Economic, Social, and Technical/Structural.
- **Barriers & Enablers:** Technical standards, regulatory policies, cost, availability, stakeholder acceptance.
- **Outcomes:** Clear synthesis, PRISMA flow documentation, quality appraisal, and actionable policy/practice recommendations.

## Pipeline & Directory Structure
1. **`01_Protocol/`**: Protocol documentation registered on OSF (`Protocol_SR_SBM_Africa_v1_2025-09-24.pdf`, `Protocol_Sustainable_Materials_Africa_2015-2025_v1.pdf`).
2. **`02_SearchLogs/`**: Search history, raw exported bibliographic results (.ris), and search logs (`SearchLog_v1_2025-09-23.csv`, `SearchLog_v2_2025-10-14.csv`, `GoogleScholar_String*.ris`).
3. **`03_Screening/`**: Title/abstract and full-text screening logs against inclusion/exclusion criteria.
4. **`04_DataExtraction/`**: Structured data extraction template (`ExtractionTemplate_v1_2025-10-14.xlsx`) and data dictionary (`Extraction_DataDictionary.txt`).
5. **`05_QualityAppraisal/`**: Critical appraisal checklists (JBI & CASP guidelines and summary ratings).
6. **`06_Analysis/`**: Thematic analysis, synthesis tables, narrative summaries, and graphical figures.
7. **`07_Manuscript/`**: Paper drafts (v1, v2) and final submission manuscripts for target peer-reviewed journals.
8. **`08_Supplements/`**: PRISMA 2020 checklist, PRISMA flow diagram template (`PRISMA2020_Flow_Template_v0.docx`), and supplementary appendices.

## Methodological Standards & Tools
- **Reporting Standard**: PRISMA 2020 (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses).
- **Quality Appraisal Frameworks**: Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) Critical Appraisal Checklists and Critical Appraisal Skills Programme (CASP).
- **Registration**: Open Science Framework (OSF).
