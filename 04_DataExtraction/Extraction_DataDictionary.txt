Extraction Data Dictionary
--------------------------

ID: Unique identifier for each study (numeric or alphanumeric).
Author: First author surname and initials.
Year: Year of publication.
Journal/Source: Journal name, conference, or repository.
Country: Country where study was conducted.
Region: African region (e.g., West Africa, East Africa).
Climate Context: Climate zone (e.g., tropical, arid, savannah).
Material Class: Broad category (Earth-based, Bio-based, Recycled, Low-carbon binder).
Material Specification: Specific material (e.g., laterite stabilized with 5% cement).
Study Aim: Authors’ stated objective.
Study Design: Experimental, observational, case study, LCA, survey, etc.
Methods Summary: Short description of methods used.
Sample Size: Number of specimens, participants, or cases.
Duration: Study or testing period (e.g., 28-day curing).
Scale: Lab, pilot, or full-scale.
Environmental Metrics: Units and methods (e.g., kg CO₂e via LCA).
Economic Metrics: Units and methods (e.g., cost per m², life-cycle cost).
Technical Metrics: Units and methods (e.g., compressive strength in MPa).
Social/Practical Notes: Adoption, skills, codes, user acceptance.
Comparator: Conventional material or alternative SBM.
Standards Referenced: Codes or standards cited (e.g., ASTM, ISO).
Key Findings: Main results as reported by authors.
Limitations: Limitations as reported or judged.
Funding Declared: Funding source, if any.
Data Availability: Whether authors provided open data.
Ethics Approval: Ethics approval status (if applicable).
Quality Tool Used: JBI, CASP, or other.
Risk of Bias: Low, Moderate, or High.
Appraisal Notes: Reviewer’s justification for bias rating.
