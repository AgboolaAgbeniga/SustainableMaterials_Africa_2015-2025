## 3. Manuscript Section Breakdown

1. **Title Page & Structured Abstract (Pages 1–2):**
   * Formal APA 7th title page, author affiliation (**Independent Researcher and Civil / Site Engineer**), ORCID link, OSF project registration, and Zenodo persistent DOI.
   * 300-word structured abstract (Background, Objectives, Methods, Results, Conclusions) and 10 keywords.
2. **Section 1: Introduction & Continental Imperative (Pages 3–8):**
   * Demographic expansion (950 million additional urban residents and 250 million housing deficit by 2050).
   * Macroeconomic and ecological crises: Clinker importation foreign exchange drain and destructive river sand dredging across the Niger, Volta, Congo, and Nile river networks.
   * The Sandcrete Collapse Mechanism: Forensic structural analysis of why commercial artisanal sandcrete blocks yield only 1.0–1.8 MPa (failing NIS 87 / GS 297 minimum standards of 2.5–2.8 MPa) and precipitate catastrophic building collapses.
   * Prespecified eligibility criteria and formal systematic review objectives.
3. **Section 2: PRISMA 2020 Systematic Methodology (Pages 8–14):**
   * Protocol registration on OSF and dual-redundant preservation on Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22771539).
   * Multi-database search architecture (Google Scholar, OpenAlex, DOAJ, AJOL; 9,112 citations; Table 2 documented multi-date trajectory).
   * Algorithmic deduplication (1,829 removed via exact DOI/title matching and fuzzy Levenshtein distance $\ge$0.90).
   * Two-stage screening protocol (4,461 excluded at Title/Abstract; 330 excluded at full text with explicit reasons; reproducible 10% re-screening audit achieving $\kappa = 0.95$).
   * Standardized 31-parameter extraction schema and multi-tool critical quality appraisal across 5 study designs.
4. **Section 3: Empirical Results & Systematic Synthesis (Pages 14–28):**
   * **3.1 Geographic Mapping:** Regional and national research concentrations *(Table 1)*.
   * **3.2 Material Taxonomy:** Proportional breakdown across 5 material classes *(Table 2)*.
   * **3.3 Earth-Based Masonry:** Compressive strength benchmarks (3.5–9.2 MPa for CSEBs vs. 1.0–1.8 MPa for sandcrete), chemical stabilization kinetics, water absorption, and thermal performance *(Table 3)*.
   * **3.4 Low-Carbon Binders & SCMs:** Limestone Calcined Clay Cement (LC3-50) hydration chemistry, carboaluminate precipitation, pore refinement, 28–52 MPa strengths, and pozzolanic reactivity of agricultural ashes.
   * **3.5 Recycled Aggregates & Industrial Wastes:** Interfacial transition zone (ITZ) modification of recycled concrete aggregates (RCA) and sand-plastic composite paving units (14–28 MPa, water absorption <1.5%).
   * **3.6 Renewable Bio-Based Materials:** Indigenous structural bamboo (*Oxytenanthera abyssinica*, *Bambusa vulgaris*), axial tensile capacities (120–240 MPa), and borax-boric acid diffusion preservation.
   * **3.7 Smart Passive Systems:** Micro-encapsulated phase change materials (PCMs) attenuating indoor diurnal temperatures by 3.5°C to 7.0°C and microbial-induced calcite precipitation (MICP).
   * **3.8 Embodied Carbon & LCA:** 30–42% embodied carbon abatement for LC3 binders, 65–78% for stabilized earth, and biogenic carbon storage potential for bamboo *(Table 4)*.
5. **Section 4: Critical Discussion, Risk of Bias & The Sandcrete Paradox (Pages 28–36):**
   * Multi-tool quality appraisal across 5 study designs (24.7% Low Risk, 75.3% Moderate Risk), with statistical variance reporting (51.7% weighted) identified as primary deficit *(Table 5)*.
   * Detailed 4-pillar Barriers-versus-Enablers Matrix across 137 adoption studies (Regulatory 78.1%, Supply Chain 71.5%, Technical 63.5%, Socio-Cultural 60.6%; non-mutually exclusive) *(Table 6)*.
   * Review limitations: Single-reviewer workflow transparently disclosed with 10% audit log, scarcity of longitudinal durability data (<15%), and foreign LCA database reliance.
6. **Section 5: Conclusions & 2026–2030 Action Roadmap (Pages 36–38):**
   * Core empirical takeaways and an actionable 5-point strategic roadmap for standards bodies (ARSO, SON, SABS, KEBS), public procurement mandates (25% green quota), PPP calcining hubs, and TVET vocational training.
7. **Declarations, References & Appendix 1 (Pages 38–48):**
   * Open science statements, CRediT author statement, funding, and competing interests.
   * 54 verified peer-reviewed references in strict APA 7th Edition format with active DOIs.
   * Appendix 1: Complete database search strings and operational execution syntax.
