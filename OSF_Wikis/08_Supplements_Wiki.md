## 1. Scope & Methodological Role
This component provides formal supplementary compliance documentation verifying that the systematic review fulfills all 27 essential items and 42 sub-items of the international **PRISMA 2020 Statement** for reporting systematic reviews.

## 3. Summary of Item-by-Item Verification

The checklist confirms comprehensive reporting across all seven PRISMA domains:

1. **Title (Item 1):** Identified explicitly as a systematic review (`Manuscript_Draft_v1.md`, Page 1).
2. **Abstract (Item 2):** 300-word structured abstract addressing Background, Objectives, Methods, Results, and Conclusions.
3. **Introduction (Items 3–4):** Continental rationale and formal PICO/CCo review objectives (Section 1.1–1.5).
4. **Methods (Items 5–15):** Complete transparency across eligibility criteria (Section 2.2), information sources (Section 2.3), search strategies (Section 2.3 & `02_SearchLogs/`), two-stage screening (Section 2.5), 31-parameter extraction schema (Section 2.6), and JBI risk of bias appraisal (Section 2.7).
5. **Results (Items 16–22):** Documented study selection (Section 3.1 & Figure 1), study characteristics across 2,492 studies (Section 3.2 & `04_DataExtraction/`), risk of bias evaluations (Section 4.1 & Table 5), and multi-dimensional syntheses (Sections 3.3–3.8 & Tables 1–4).
6. **Discussion (Item 23a–d):** General interpretation of structural and environmental findings, explicit limitations (lack of longitudinal durability data and foreign LCA background reliance), and policy/regulatory implications (Section 4.1–4.4 & Section 5.2).
7. **Other Information (Items 24–27):** OSF registration and Zenodo persistent DOI (Item 24), CRediT author statement (Item 25), zero external funding declaration (Item 26), and competing interests declaration (Item 27).
