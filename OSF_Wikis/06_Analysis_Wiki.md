## 1. Scope & Analytical Overview
This component presents the synthesized empirical evidence base. It integrates multi-dimensional quantitative analyses spanning geographic representation, taxonomic distribution, mechanical performance benchmarks, life cycle assessment (LCA) environmental profiles, empirical adoption barriers, and methodological quality appraisal across 2,492 studies.

---

## 2. Synthesis Tables Structure (`Synthesis_Tables.xlsx`)

The workbook `Synthesis_Tables.xlsx` contains six interconnected analytical worksheets:

1. **Sheet 1: Geographic_Distribution:** Country-by-country study frequencies, regional market shares (West Africa 38.2%, East Africa 21.4%, Southern Africa 18.6%, North Africa 14.1%, Central Africa 7.7%), and cross-border research collaborations.
2. **Sheet 2: Taxonomy_Breakdown:** Granular breakdown of the five material classes, sub-material classifications (e.g., CSEBs, LC3, geopolymer, bio-composite), and specific raw material feedstocks.
3. **Sheet 3: Mechanical_Benchmarks:** Compressive strength (mean, min, max, standard deviation), flexural strength, tensile strength, dry density, water absorption, and thermal conductivity benchmarked against international standards (BS EN 206, ASTM C618, ARS 680, NIS 87, GS 297).
4. **Sheet 4: LCA_Environmental_Profiles:** Embodied carbon (kg CO₂-eq/m³), embodied energy (MJ/m³), percentage carbon reductions relative to Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) concrete and artisanal fired bricks, and net biogenic carbon sequestration dynamics.
5. **Sheet 5: Barriers_Enablers_Matrix:** Empirical frequency of systemic adoption bottlenecks (Regulatory 78%, Supply Chain 72%, Technical 64%, Socio-Cultural 61%) mapped directly to validated engineering and policy enablers.
6. **Sheet 6: JBI_Quality_Summary:** Itemized compliance percentages across the 9 Joanna Briggs Institute criteria categorized by material class.
