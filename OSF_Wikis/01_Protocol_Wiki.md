## 1. Scope & Objective
This component houses the foundational systematic review protocol registered prior to full-text data extraction. The protocol defines the operational scope, methodological boundaries, review questions, and eligibility criteria formulated in accordance with the PRISMA-P (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analysis Protocols) guidelines and the Population/Context, Intervention/Phenomenon, Comparator, and Outcomes (PICO/CCo) framework.

---

## 2. Methodological Governance & PICO/CCo Criteria

* **Population / Context (Context):** Sovereign territories of the African continent across five geographic regions (North, West, Central, East, and Southern Africa), encompassing tropical wet, semi-arid Sahelian, Mediterranean, and subtropical highland climatic zones.
* **Intervention / Phenomenon of Interest:** Empirical laboratory investigations, prototype testing, life-cycle assessments (LCA), or field demonstration trials of sustainable building materials belonging to five defined taxonomic classes:
  1. Earth-Based Masonry (CEB, CSEB, rammed earth, stabilized laterite, adobe);
  2. Low-Carbon Binders (LC3, geopolymers, calcined clays, agricultural pozzolanic ashes);
  3. Recycled & Industrial Wastes (recycled concrete aggregate, waste plastics, crushed glass, slag);
  4. Renewable Bio-Based Systems (structural bamboo, mass timber, vegetable fiber composites);
  5. Smart & Innovative Materials (phase change materials, microbial-induced calcite precipitation).
* **Comparator:** Conventional carbon-intensive baselines prevalent in African construction: Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) concrete conforming to BS EN 206 C20/25, commercial sandcrete hollow blocks conforming to Nigerian NIS 87 or Ghanaian GS 297, and clamp-fired artisanal clay bricks.
* **Outcomes:** 
  - *Mechanical & Physical:* 28-day Compressive Strength (MPa), Flexural/Tensile Strength (MPa), Bulk Dry Density (kg/m³), Water Absorption (%), Thermal Conductivity (W/m·K).
  - *Environmental:* Embodied Carbon (kg CO₂-eq/m³ or kg CO₂-eq/tonne), Global Warming Potential (GWP) % reduction, Embodied Energy (MJ/m³).
  - *Systemic Adoption:* Empirical prevalence of institutional, economic, technical, and socio-cultural adoption barriers vs. empirical policy/engineering enablers.
